Spotting Market Leaders

Entry flags under the candles — what each one means
▲P Pocket pivot — an early buy inside a base, or off the 10-day in an uptrend, before the obvious breakout (Morales & Kacher). An up day (close above the prior close) whose volume is at least the largest down-day volume of the prior 10 sessions, closing above the 50-day average, with the day's low right at or just below the 10-day average (at most 1% above it) and the close back above the 10-day.
▲G Gap-up buy point — opens above the prior day's high; the gap from the prior close is at least 0.75× the average true range of the previous 40 sessions; volume is at least 1.5× its 50-day average; and the day's range comes within 5% of the base high. Stop = the gap day's low.
▲B Breakout — the first close above the base high (the highest high of the prior 6 months, set at least 3 weeks earlier) on volume at least 1.5× its 50-day average — the book's 50%-above-average breakout rule.
△ Not a buy — a pocket pivot or gap that breaks one of the book's rules — extended (low more than 1% above the 10-day), closed below the 10-day, below the 50-day, a downtrend (50-day falling, under the 200-day), the lower third of the base, wedging up on lighter volume, a V-shaped drop to the 20-day that never touched the 50-day, a wide and loose base (deeper than 50% or weekly swings of 10%+), or a gap extended more than 5% above the base high or still inside the base. Shown for the last 60 sessions only.
The flags are judged on SIMPLE moving averages, as in the book's charts: the 10-day (location, extended) and 50-day (above it), plus the 20-day (V-shape) and 200-day (downtrend). Every chart shows the 10- and 50-day lines — dotted where the page's own lines are EMAs.
Hover a marker to see its numbers, whether the day closed in the upper half of its range (the book calls a mid-range close weak) and whether trading was quiet before it. The thresholds freeze a discretionary book (Trade Like an O'Neil Disciple, ch. 5) into rules: reading aids, not tested signals. On NSE only the breakout-day versions are measured — gap-ups helped (42.3% vs 34.5% reached +20% in 60 days); pocket-pivot volume barely did, because 77% of breakouts have it.
