📖 Column Guide — What Each Metric Means
Every metric shown on the stock cards, explained. Gates marked in amber are hard filters — stock must pass to appear here.
GSI Status (pill: Forming Base / Buy Zone)
Hard gate: must be Forming Base or Buy Zone in BOTH GSI and today's timeseries
MarketSmith's assessment of where the stock is in its base cycle. Forming Base = stock is still building the base — the optimal low-risk entry window. Buy Zone = base is complete, stock is in the buyable range just above the pivot. Complete = base done but stock has moved, risk is higher — excluded from this report.
Forming Base is priority. AZAD was Forming Base on Jun 29 — returned +22.7% in 10 days.
AI (pill: High / Med / Low)
Display only — no hard gate
MarketSmith AI conviction verdict on this GSI entry. High = strong institutional conviction signal, multiple confirming factors. Med = moderate conviction, valid setup but fewer confirmations. Low = weak conviction — treat with extra caution.
Elite combo: AI=High + prob≥75% + RS 95+ → mean +10% in 10 days (backtested).
Prob % (pill: e.g. 65%)
Hard gate: ≥ 60%
GSI's probability score for this entry being a successful breakout. Higher = more confident. Scores of 75%+ combined with AI=High have historically produced the best outcomes.
AZAD had 65% prob — mid-tier. NATIONALUM and LUMAXTECH have 85% — top tier.
RS (Relative Strength Rating)
Hard gate: 75 – 94
How the stock has performed vs all other stocks over the last 12 months, on a 1–99 scale. 99 = top 1%. We cap at 94 because RS 95+ stocks are often already extended — the pre-breakout sweet spot is 75–94 with RS rising.
AZAD was RS 85 on entry. RS rose to 92 as price moved — confirms institutional buying.
RS 2W (2-week RS delta)
Hard gate: ≥ −3 (RS must be stable or rising)
How much the RS Rating has changed in the last 2 weeks (e.g. +12 means RS rose 12 points). A rising RS while price is still in the base = institutions are accumulating quietly before the breakout. A falling RS = distribution — avoid.
Green sparkline = RS rising. Flat or rising RS inside a base is the strongest pre-breakout signal.
EPS (Earnings Per Share Rating)
Hard gate: ≥ 75
Composite score of recent EPS growth (last 2 quarters) + annual EPS trend on a 1–99 scale. Ensures the stock has real fundamental earnings momentum behind the price move.
EPS 75+ = earnings are accelerating or strong. Below 75 = stock may be running on hype.
Comp (Composite Strength)
Hard gate: ≥ 82
MarketSmith's overall fundamental score combining EPS growth, sales growth, profit margins, and ROE on a 1–99 scale. A high Composite means the business itself is in excellent shape — not just the stock price.
Composite 90+ = elite fundamentals. QPOWER, POLYCAB, LUMAXTECH are all 96–99.
Group # (Industry Group Rank)
Hard gate: ≤ 100 (top half of ~197 groups)
Where this stock's industry group ranks among all MarketSmith industry groups by recent price performance. Group #1 = strongest group. Stocks in rising groups get a sector tailwind — a rising tide lifts all boats. Stocks in groups #100+ face a structural headwind.
QPOWER, POLYCAB are in Group #28 (Electrical-Power) — one of the hottest groups right now.
Blue Dots
Display only — removed as hard gate (was too restrictive)
Count of "blue dot" accumulation days — days where the stock closed up on significantly above-average volume, a sign of institutional buying. More dots = more institutional demand. Zero dots doesn't mean no accumulation — it may just not have had a big-volume up day yet.
PICCADIL has 8 blue dots = heavy accumulation. QPOWER has 0 but Composite=99 — institutions are still in it.
vs 52H (% from 52-Week High)
Hard gate: −35% to −2%
How far below the 52-week high the stock is currently trading. −14% = stock is 14% below its 52W high, still in base territory. −2% = near breakout point. −35% = deeper base, more work needed. We exclude stocks at 0% (at highs = extended) or beyond −35% (too deep, broken structure).
AZAD was at −14% on entry. The shallower the base (−5% to −15%), the closer to breakout.
Price
Display only
Current market price of the stock in INR (₹). Useful for position sizing — higher-priced stocks require larger capital per share for the same position value.
No gate on price — focus on MCap for size filter instead.
MCap (Market Capitalisation)
Hard gate: ≥ 300 Cr
Total market value of the company in Crores (₹). We require at least ₹300 Cr to avoid illiquid micro-caps where institutions cannot take meaningful positions. Shown as kCr (thousands of crores) for large-caps.
₹300 Cr minimum ensures enough liquidity for institutional participation — a prerequisite for sustained moves.
RS 6W Sparkline
Display only
Mini chart showing how the RS Rating has trended over the last 6 weeks (weekly snapshots). Green rising line = RS accelerating upward while stock is still in base = best signal. Red falling line = RS deteriorating = caution.
A rising RS sparkline inside a Forming Base = the best pre-breakout setup we look for.
AZAD Score (ring number)
Display only — ranks stocks within the filtered list
Our composite similarity score to the AZAD entry profile (the backtest template). Combines RS (25%), Composite (20%), EPS (15%), Group rank (15%), RS momentum (12%), probability (8%), AI verdict (5%), base shallowness bonus. Higher = more AZAD-like. ≥85 = elite, 70–84 = high, <70 = moderate.
Green ring = elite (≥85), amber ring = high (70–84), grey ring = moderate (<70).
GSI Entered (days ago)
Display only
How many trading days ago this stock first appeared in the GSI watchlist. Earlier entries (fewer days ago) are fresher signals. Stocks that entered 30+ days ago and are still in Forming Base may be taking longer to set up — check if the base is still healthy.
Fresh entries (<10 days) are generally higher priority — the signal is more current.
Entry Trigger
Display only — actionable guidance
Suggested entry condition based on the base type. Forming Base: watch for volume dry-up (3–4 consecutive low-volume days) followed by a bounce, or a range expansion day on 2× average volume. Buy Zone: enter on a pullback to the 21EMA or 10EMA, or on next volume expansion above the pivot.
Never chase — wait for the trigger. AZAD's day-1 dip was the entry, not the high.
