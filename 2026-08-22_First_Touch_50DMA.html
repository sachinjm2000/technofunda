Method

Stage 1 (MarketSmith snapshot fields only, no price fetch): the 50DMA and 52-week-high price levels are back-computed from Pct_Chg_vs_50DMA and Pct_from_52W_High, then filtered to stocks touching the 50DMA now, with the 52W-high sitting ≥20% above it (a real prior rally), RS≥80, Composite≥80, Group Rank≤60.

Stage 2 (actual daily OHLC, survivors only): confirms rising 50DMA + above 200DMA, then scans up to 252 prior sessions for touch events — a stock is "first touch" only if no earlier touch occurred since its current up-leg began. Any prior touch (this leg or before) that closed ≤-5% below the 50DMA within 10 sessions is logged as rejected. MA confluence = the spread across 10DMA/21EMA/50DMA as a %% of price, tight when ≤4%.

Caveats

  • Stage 1's 50DMA/52W-high levels are back-computed estimates from MSI's percentage fields, not the exact chart values — Stage 2 recomputes both precisely from real OHLC for anything shown here.
  • "Touching" uses the session's low/high range, not just the close — a name can show as touching even if it closed well above the line.
  • In-sample thresholds (touch band, min-gap, confluence-max) are reasoned defaults, not backtested for this specific screen — see wiki/strategies/Buying-Pullbacks.md for the related backtests that are.
