Method: how these stocks are listed and filtered

1. Universe

Union of stocks on these IBD lists (one row per symbol; the Lists column shows every list it is on): IBD 50, Sector Leaders, Big Cap 20, IPO Leaders, Stock Spotlight, New Highs, RS Line At New High, Rising Profit Estimates, Stocks Funds Are Buying, Your Weekly Review, Stocks On The Move. Not included yet: Long-Term Leaders, Stocks Near Buy Zone, Breakout Stocks Index. Lists are small when the market is weak, so the universe is not the whole market.

2. Data per stock

  • IBD Stock Checkup (collected from a signed-in IBD session): Composite, EPS, RS, SMR, Acc/Dis, industry group rank (of 142) and group RS, EPS and sales growth, % off 52-week high, vs 50-day average. Values are as of collection time, so they can be intraday.
  • RMV15: computed here from daily price bars (Yahoo chart data) with Deepvue's published RMV formula, 15-bar window, on the last completed session (the still-forming day is dropped). Lower = tighter recent range.
  • Anything missing shows [DATA NEEDED] and never passes a filter. Nothing is estimated.

3. Preset filters (click to turn on; several on = all must pass)

  • IBD checklist: Composite ≥ 95, EPS Rating ≥ 80, RS Rating ≥ 80, SMR A or B, Acc/Dis A to D. Thresholds are from IBD's own "how to buy stocks" guide; any B variant (B-, B, B+) counts as B.
  • Leaders in top-40 groups: IBD industry group rank ≤ 40 of 142. The cut-off of 40 is my choice, mirroring the xang1234 scanner's "leaders in leading groups" idea; it is not an IBD rule.
  • Tight (RMV): RMV15 ≤ 10, Deepvue's "Tight Range" preset value. It is not backtested.
  • Within 5% of high / Within 10% of high: price no more than 5% (or 10%) below the 52-week high. These are convenience cut-offs, not IBD rules.

3b. Column filters (the box under each header)

  • Number columns: >=80, <10, =95, a range 5..9, or ? for missing. A box that cannot be read turns red and is ignored.
  • Text columns (symbol, company, lists, grades): case-insensitive "contains", e.g. A matches A, A+ and A-; ? for missing.
  • Column filters, preset chips and the search box all combine (every one must pass). "Clear column filters" resets the boxes.

4. What this page does not do

  • No blended score and no ranking: sort any column yourself.
  • It is a screen, not a buy signal. It does not check base shape, buy point, volume on the breakout or earnings dates. IBD's market exposure is shown in the banner; when the market is in a downtrend, new entries are not advised.
  • Presets are applied in your browser from the same rules as the build script; this is not investment advice.
