What the labels mean, and what this page is not

5M TT / 1M TT / None — Minervini Trend Template criterion 3 only (200-day SMA trending up), measured at month-end checkpoints, the same convention the Minervini-1M TT and Minervini-5M TT tabs use. 5M TT = the 200-day SMA is higher at each of the last five month-end checkpoints. 1M TT = higher than a month ago but not the full five. None = not rising month-over-month. A recent listing without five checkpoints is labelled with an explicit short-history note and is never upgraded to 5M TT on partial data.

This is not a screen and not a recommendation. It is the watchlist rendered as charts so a setup can be eyeballed. Nothing here has passed the SPEC.md Tier 1 gates — those are built on MarketSmith ratings (RS, EPS, Composite, A/D, Group Rank) that do not exist for US names. Treat the Trend Template label as the only computed claim on this page; everything else is a picture.

Volume colouring (TraderLion): green = high relative volume with a strong close, blue = up day, magenta = down day, orange = low relative volume. RS line (Jack Corsellis): price ÷ SPY, with a blue dot marking a 52-week RS-line high.
