How this list is built

1. Quality gate — identical to the pullback watchlist

Composite ≥ 80, RS ≥ 70, SMR ≥ 70, industry group rank ≤ 60, A/D ≥ B-. Applied to today's ratings, so a name whose ratings have decayed drops out.

2. Base still Forming

MarketSmith must still report Base_Status_Daily == "Forming". A stock already past its pivot is answering a different question and belongs in the pullback watchlist instead.

3. A VALIDATED base low

MarketSmith publishes distance from the pivot (the base's top), never from its low, so the low is computed from price history. Walking backward from today, the base's high is the most recent point price has retraced ≥ 12% from — O'Neil's minimum base depth. That threshold is what stops a noise-level wiggle inside an ongoing rally from posing as a base: a naive "highest close in the window" first cut returned stocks already 50–100% past their real pivot. Price must then sit 5–15% above that low.

4. The reversal candle

The same three mandatory conditions as the pullback watchlist (upside_reversal.check_conditions), fired within 3 sessions: low tests a support level and the close reclaims it (or the low undercuts the prior low while price is not extended); close at/above the prior close or an intraday reclaim of that level; close in the upper half of the range. Validated 48/48 against Harris's own marked candles. When several bars fire consecutively the first of the run is reported — the later ones carry a far worse stop.

Why the ordering is by distance from the low

Not a quality ranking. The stop is the reversal candle's low, so a name nearer the base floor simply risks less if the base fails. Read top-down for risk, not for conviction.
