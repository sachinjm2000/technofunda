How to use a fresh detection (the guide these checkpoints come from)
1 · Earliest, least-proven signal. A group detected today is at the start of Minervini's "stocks point to the group" process, before it looks obvious — the best time to notice it, the worst time to trust it.
2 · Look before it's hot. Open the group via its MarketSmith link; check its top 2–3 stocks by RS, how close they are to their highs, and their earnings. Most names start as EARLY or WATCH, not PRIME — correct.
3 · Watch whether it holds. One day is fragile: only ~30% of state changes follow the ladder. After 2–3 sessions: still tracked (the 3-session grace rule)? After ~5: new highs rising and 5D accel positive? After ~10–20: read Stock since det. for each member.
4 · Let "since detection" pick the real leaders. After 2–4 weeks the members with the largest excess return since detection are the ones that actually led once the signal appeared — not just today's highest RS (Chemicals-Specialty: BODALCHEM and SETL +100%+ vs FCL +35%).
5 · Compare with mature groups. Young (0–10 sessions): early, higher risk of flipping back, most room to run. Mature (40+): proven but often extended — breakouts inside Strong groups did worse than in Leading / Emerging ones in the archive walk-forward.
Rule of thumb: a group detected today goes on your radar, not into a buy. Buying still needs a PRIME stock near its trigger and the market gate open.
